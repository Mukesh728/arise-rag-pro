Ingestion & chunking

Why ~500 tokens with 50 overlap?

A 500-token window keeps a full definition or paragraph together, while the 50-token overlap stops concepts being cut mid-sentence at chunk boundaries.

How are citations grounded by page?

pypdf records the page of every extracted range. Each chunk carries page_number in its Chroma metadata, so citations map back to the exact textbook page.

Dual indexing

Chunks are stored in ChromaDB for semantic retrieval and registered in the BM25 Okapi index for keyword retrieval — at the same time.

    Deep dive

    Why Hybrid RRF wins

    Dense vectors match paraphrases but can miss exact terms like "FCFS", "HRRN" or "SCAN".

    BM25 nails technical keywords and course codes but misses semantic variations.

    RRF merges both lists by rank: Σ 1 / (60 + rank(d)) — no score calibration needed.
